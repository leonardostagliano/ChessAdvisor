ChessAdvisor bundles data derived from open Lichess datasets in
`resources/data`:

- `puzzles.json` — derived from the Lichess puzzle database
  (https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles)
- `openings.json` — derived from lichess-org/chess-openings
  (https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings); the EPD of each position is
  computed by ChessAdvisor's own preparation script
- `endgames.json` — derived from the same public-domain Lichess material

These datasets are released by Lichess under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0
Universal Public Domain Dedication. To the extent possible under law, the
authors have waived all copyright and related or neighbouring rights to this
material worldwide.
